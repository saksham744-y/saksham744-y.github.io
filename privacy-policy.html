Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Daybreak Planner handles and how. Last updated 2026-11-03.

Who we are

Daybreak Planner is published by Saksham Yadav. You can contact us at sakshamy744@gmail.com.

Your plan stays on your device

Your tasks, ticks, day notes and settings are saved in your browser’s local storage on your own device. We do not run accounts, and we do not receive or store your plan on any server. Clearing your browser’s site data, or using Reset on the Function tab, removes this information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the browser or device shows reminders for your tasks. These reminders are created on your device from the plan stored there. They are not sent to our servers.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard technical logs of requests made to its servers, such as the address requested and the time. Please see GitHub’s privacy statement for how it handles that information.

Advertising and cookies

We plan to show advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn about Google’s advertising cookies and choose not to receive personalised ads by visiting Google’s Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com. You can also visit aboutads.info to manage cookies from participating companies. Where a consent message is required in your region, we will show it before ads load.

The Android app

The Android version of Daybreak keeps its data on your phone. It does not require an account.

Children

Daybreak is a general planner and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
