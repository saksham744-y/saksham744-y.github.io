About Daybreak Planner

Daybreak is a free planner that turns a plain list of tasks into a timed schedule for your day.

What it does

You type your tasks as you would say them, such as “Maths 9 to 10 am” or “sleep 8pm”. Daybreak reads the times, builds a schedule in time order, and shows what is happening now and what comes next. You can repeat tasks on the days of the week you choose, add a task to a specific date, write a note for each day, and tick tasks off as you finish them.

How it works

Daybreak runs in your web browser and can be added to your home screen. There is no account. Your plan is stored in your browser on your device. The planner does not send your tasks to a server.

Who made it

Daybreak Planner is made by Saksham Yadav. It is built to be simple, fast and private.
