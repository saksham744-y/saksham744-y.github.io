What is Daybreak?

Daybreak is a free daily planner. Type your tasks in the Add tab, for example “Maths 9 to 10 am” or “sleep 8pm”, and it builds a timed schedule for your day. You get a reminder before each task, notes for every date, and a tick sound when you finish something.

Tasks can repeat on the days of the week you choose, or be added to specific dates. Your plan is saved in your browser, so there is no account to create.
